Why Data Engineering Matters for Managers and Business Owners — Part 1

Why, in Iran's current economy, business owners should think about hiring a 'data engineer' before hiring any analyst if they want to use their data.

Where does the problem start?#

Have you ever looked at financial and sales reports that seemed accurate and polished, yet you doubted whether the numbers were actually correct?

Usually the problem starts here: the data collected for analysis is incomplete, error-prone, or out of date. In that situation, many businesses look for the fix in hiring more analysts — while the real root of the problem is somewhere else.

What is a data engineer’s real role?#

A data engineer is responsible for designing and building the data infrastructure. They manage moving data from different sources (sales, finance, inventory, the website) and make sure the data is correct, unified, and available on time.

Then data analysts, analytics engineers, data scientists, and BI developers use that prepared data to produce reports, models, and analyses.

Reporting on top of incorrect data is like building a house on water.

That’s exactly why the data engineer’s role in creating this kind of infrastructure is so critical.

Why is a data engineer vital in Iran’s volatile economy?#

Timely decision-making When prices change quickly, you need to know the profitability of your products in the shortest possible time. A delay in accessing data means missed opportunities.

Financial transparency Under inflation, the sales figure is deceptive. Your sales may have grown 300%, yet your profit is negative! By integrating financial and operational data, a data engineer makes it possible to calculate the “real profit” of each product.

Fewer costly mistakes Incorrect data leads to wrong decisions: over-purchasing, mispriced discounts, and untargeted advertising.

A practical approach#

  • First priority — data infrastructure: Before investing in advanced reporting tools, strengthen your data infrastructure.
  • Define the key needs: Define the key performance indicators (KPIs) for your data engineer, and appoint someone to keep them close to the business.
  • Analysis comes next: Once a reliable data infrastructure is in place, hiring analysts can create real value.

In today’s Iranian economy, a durable competitive advantage belongs to businesses that make decisions based on correct, timely, and unified data.
