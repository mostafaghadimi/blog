---

## The real challenges of building a data team

In practice, HR managers, team leads, and business owners run into the following challenges:

### 1. Long time to reliable data

Building a data team and standing up the right infrastructure takes time. When decision speed is what matters most, that delay can mean losing important decision-making opportunities.

### 2. The talent shortage

- Genuinely senior people are extremely scarce.
- One senior person alone isn't enough — you need a team.
- Finding someone who has both the technical ability and the team-leadership skills is extremely hard.

### 3. Retention

Given the better financial packages larger companies offer, retaining specialized talent has become a serious challenge.

### 4. High key-person risk

For smaller companies, the departure of one key person can mean:

- Losing the architecture that was designed
- No longer being able to maintain and evolve the system
- Wasting the money and time already invested

### 5. The high cost of a full team

Data engineering is not a one- or two-person job. To stand up proper data infrastructure, you need a team made up of several specialists across different areas.

---

## An alternative: outsourcing

Given the challenges above, outsourcing your data needs can be an alternative to building a team — but there are considerations to keep in mind.

The most important considerations when outsourcing data projects to companies/startups:

- **Protecting sensitive data** (PII)
- **Time to an operational result**
- **Confidence in the correctness of the outputs**
- **Ongoing support**
- **The ability to onboard the company's current technical staff** and share knowledge
- **Flexibility** in evolving and adding new capabilities
